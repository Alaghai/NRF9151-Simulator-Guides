DOCUMENT EDIT REPORT

Editing approach
----------------
The three supplied guides were preserved. Changes were kept local and focused
on short additions, table rows, command examples, and the minimum replacement
of obsolete ZIP-based installation instructions. All final DOCX files were
rendered to page images and visually inspected.
